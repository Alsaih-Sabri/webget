# The Ultimate SEO Guide for 2024

Search engine optimization continues to evolve, and staying ahead requires understanding the latest best practices and algorithm updates.

## Core Web Vitals Matter More Than Ever

Google's emphasis on user experience means your site's performance directly impacts rankings:

- **LCP (Largest Contentful Paint)** - Under 2.5 seconds
- **FID (First Input Delay)** - Under 100 milliseconds  
- **CLS (Cumulative Layout Shift)** - Under 0.1

## Content Quality Over Quantity

The days of keyword stuffing are long gone. Focus on:

1. **E-E-A-T** - Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness
2. **User Intent** - Match content to what users actually want
3. **Comprehensive Coverage** - Be the definitive resource on your topic

## Technical SEO Fundamentals

Don't overlook the basics:

- Clean URL structure
- Proper heading hierarchy
- Schema markup
- XML sitemaps
- Mobile-first design

## Conclusion

SEO success in 2024 comes from combining technical excellence with genuinely valuable content that serves your audience's needs.
