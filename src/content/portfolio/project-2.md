# Brand Identity Design for InnovateTech

## Overview

InnovateTech needed a fresh brand identity that would position them as a modern, innovative technology company while maintaining approachability for their diverse customer base.

## Deliverables

- **Logo Design** - Modern, scalable logo with multiple variations
- **Color Palette** - Primary and secondary color schemes
- **Typography** - Font selection and usage guidelines
- **Brand Guidelines** - Comprehensive 40-page brand book
- **Stationery** - Business cards, letterheads, and email signatures

## The Result

A cohesive brand identity that has helped InnovateTech stand out in a crowded market and establish trust with their customers.
